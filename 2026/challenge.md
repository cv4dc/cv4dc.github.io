CV4DC 2026 will host the first challenge of the workshop series, on **resource-constrained computer vision for applications in developing regions**.

The full call will be published on this page, and the results and winning methods will be presented during the challenge session at the workshop in Osaka.

If you would like to be notified when the challenge opens, please get in touch.
